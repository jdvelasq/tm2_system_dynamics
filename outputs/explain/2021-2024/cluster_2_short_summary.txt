This thematic cluster is defined by the application of system dynamics modeling to the pressing, multi-scalar challenges at the nexus of urban development, environmental quality, and global sustainability, with a particular focus on carbon cycles, greenhouse gas and air pollutant emissions, landfill management, and the evolution of smart urban infrastructure [UT 368, UT 188, UT 341, UT 275, UT 14, UT 243, UT 485, UT 387, UT 494, UT 201, UT 44, UT 486, UT 328, UT 336, UT 40, UT 168, UT 310, UT 335, UT 468, UT 271, UT 107, UT 377, UT 35, UT 244, UT 129, UT 493, UT 333, UT 434, UT 41, UT 417, UT 54, UT 56, UT 95, UT 346, UT 469, UT 155, UT 42, UT 364, UT 466, UT 422, UT 337, UT 252, UT 349, UT 68, UT 405]. Recent trends illustrate the increasing conceptual and methodological sophistication of system dynamics, as empirical, simulation-based and scenario-oriented studies are increasingly deployed to manage feedback-driven complexities across energy use, public policy, urban transportation, resource flows, and citizen wellbeing within urban and global domains [UT 341, UT 14, UT 275, UT 485, UT 95, UT 346, UT 155, UT 42, UT 107, UT 377, UT 35]. However, significant challenges persist: conceptual integration of the many intertwined subsystems remains hampered by disciplinary silos and the complexity of modeling overlapping socio-economic, technological, and behavioral factors, while methodological obstacles such as data scarcity, model validation, boundary setting, and parameter uncertainty limit robust generalization and operational value [UT 387, UT 328, UT 336, UT 335, UT 244, UT 252, UT 368, UT 188, UT 275, UT 35, UT 377, UT 337, UT 405, UT 68, UT 41, UT 341, UT 485, UT 14, UT 243, UT 44, UT 40, UT 168, UT 271, UT 336, UT 493, UT 107, UT 129, UT 434]. Future research opportunities abound, spanning the integration of multi-scalar and real-time data, hybridization of qualitative and quantitative approaches, improved geospatial and semantic coupling, inclusion of informal sector dynamics, and the design of adaptive policy testing frameworks to foster resilient, low-carbon, and equitable urban futures [UT 188, UT 275, UT 486, UT 468, UT 201, UT 387, UT 364, UT 41, UT 244, UT 377, UT 493, UT 341, UT 485, UT 44, UT 168, UT 271, UT 54, UT 95, UT 335, UT 40, UT 107, UT 129, UT 252, UT 68, UT 39, UT 243, UT 368, UT 405, UT 422, UT 466].

This thematic cluster is defined by the application of system dynamics approaches to model and manage the complex, interconnected challenges of waste generation, recycling, environmental management, energy consumption, landfill utilization, and public health—particularly acute in rapidly urbanizing contexts such as Shanghai [UT 72][UT 109][UT 998][UT 624][UT 860][UT 584][UT 952][UT 610][UT 501][UT 894][UT 838][UT 713][UT 836][UT 628][UT 630][UT 844][UT 864][UT 728][UT 591][UT 977][UT 549][UT 815][UT 675][UT 885][UT 875]. Recent research trends reveal an increasing emphasis on scenario-based simulation to assess environmental, economic, and public health outcomes under diverse policy paradigms, including circular economy strategies, with system dynamics emerging as the dominant methodology for integrating feedback-rich relationships across sectors and spatial scales [UT 72][UT 109][UT 831][UT 998][UT 624][UT 876][UT 860][UT 584][UT 610][UT 844][UT 838][UT 894][UT 501][UT 836][UT 864][UT 591][UT 728][UT 779][UT 854][UT 890][UT 782][UT 977][UT 549][UT 952]. Analysis now extends from traditional municipal, construction, and demolition waste to electronic waste, battery reuse, and disease transmission risks associated with landfill and improper waste handling, illustrating the growing sophistication and transdisciplinarity of this field. Nevertheless, challenges persist: the intricacy of modeling multifaceted waste, recycle, and environmental systems complicates system boundary definitions, integration of diverse and often incomplete data sources, and incorporation of behavioral, institutional, and informal sector dynamics—all of which undermine model calibration and limit generalizability, particularly where regulatory or technological inertia and sociocultural barriers persist [UT 836][UT 838][UT 549][UT 977][UT 885][UT 875][UT 72][UT 109][UT 894][UT 610][UT 860][UT 779][UT 728][UT 815]. Practical advancements will require embedding system dynamics within decision-support systems, aligning models with operational metrics and regulatory frameworks, and enhancing empirical monitoring capacity to facilitate real-time calibration and actionable insights for waste, energy, environmental, and disease management [UT 998][UT 624][UT 864][UT 728][UT 885][UT 728][UT 890][UT 815][UT 836][UT 844][UT 501][UT 610][UT 675][UT 72][UT 682][UT 584][UT 952][UT 894][UT 628].
